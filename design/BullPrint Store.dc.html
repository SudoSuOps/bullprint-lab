THE CONSTRAINT NOBODY MENTIONS

Printing is the cheap part. Inspection, finishing, serialising, packing and support scale with people, not printers. Every product below is scored on post-processing minutes, because that number — not print time — is what caps the store.

WHY A CUP AND NOT A SECOND INSOLE

It slides under a footbed the wearer already likes instead of replacing it, so it fits boots and dress shoes where a full-length insole crowds the toe box. Same profile data, half the print time.

WHAT CHANGES FROM THE INSOLE

Wall goes up to 22 mm to actually hold the heel. Cells close to 5.5 mm under the pad where the load lands, open to 8.0 mm through the arch. The forefoot simply doesn't exist — the cut feathers to 1.8 mm so nothing catches underfoot.

STILL TO PROVE

Whether the feathered cut survives repeated insertion into a tight shoe. That is the bull-off risk on this one, and it is why the cup sits in the pipeline rather than in a drop slot.

THE RULE

One finish per object — no two-tone prints, no colour-change gimmicks. The bull mark is always gold or debossed, whatever the body colour, so the identity survives the palette. Bitcoin orange is a filament we do not stock: ₿ appears as geometry, never as a colour.

AVOID

Rainbow, silk, glitter and colour-shift filaments. They print beautifully and they make the object look like a hobby print — which is the exact impression the whole brand is built to avoid.

PRICING LOGIC

Price on finishing time and design value, never on filament weight. A $2 spool cost tells a customer nothing and prices the work at zero. Every item carries its USD price and a sats figure at spot — display only, unless we ship quoted BTC pricing with an expiry.

WHAT WOULD BREAK THIS

Selling anything that carries a safety burden we can't back with testing — orthotic claims, protective footwear, climbing or safety toe. A footbed that fails is an uncomfortable afternoon. Keep the line there — nothing structural, nothing protective, nothing a person would trust with their safety.
