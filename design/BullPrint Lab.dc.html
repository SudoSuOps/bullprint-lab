02 — WHAT IS A BULLPRINT?

NOT A STICKER.
NOT A LOGO SLAPPED ON A PRODUCT.
THE EXPRESSION IS BUILT INTO THE PRINT.

A BullPrint integrates expression directly into geometry, material, texture and function. The ₿ is not printed on the insert — it is a region of the lattice where the cells close up, stiffen and become a symbol. Remove the graphic and you remove structure.

The identity should feel native to the object. Nothing peels, nothing wears off, and nothing about it reads as decoration bolted onto a part that was finished without it.
