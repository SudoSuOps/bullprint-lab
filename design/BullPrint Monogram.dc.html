THE HONEST CAVEAT

This needs a cut-and-sew partner, which you don't have yet. It is not a printing problem, so nothing on your floor makes it. Price it once you have a quote for 200 units, not before.

SENDING THIS TO A FABRIC PRINTER

Give them the repeat tile, not a swatch — one tile at 300 dpi with the repeat type named (all four here are straight repeats). Gold on a 3 mm spacer knit will sink into the pile, so ask for a pigment or sublimation test on the actual mesh before committing a roll: the same file that looks sharp on a flat woven goes soft on an open-cell knit. Keep text above 8 pt on the finished cloth.

HOW THE RELIEF PRINTS

A 0.6 mm deboss at 0.20 mm layer height is three layers deep — enough to hold a shadow, shallow enough that the band still flexes across it. Keep the stroke at 1.2 mm, three nozzle widths, or the walls between motifs get fragile. The wordmark goes the other way: raised 0.8 mm so it stays proud of the pattern and reads first.

MY CALL

1a for the drop. It carries both of your symbols and earns the monogram read honestly — the ₿ in the repeat is the same argument the insole makes, expression built into geometry rather than laid on top. 1b is the better pure texture and the stronger second colourway; I'd keep it for the swims, where a quieter pattern suits a wet-environment shoe.
