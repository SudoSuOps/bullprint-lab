THE RISK ON THIS CONSTRUCTION

Three parts means three chances for tolerance to stack. If BASE-2 prints 0.3 proud and the tab pocket prints 0.3 tight, the band binds and the assembly stops closing flat. Print one full set and measure the pocket before committing a batch — that gate is step 5.
