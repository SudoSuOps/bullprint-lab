SHEET 07

VOICE

The humour gets attention. The quality earns the customer. Both of those are true at once, and the order matters — we open with the joke and close with the spec, never the reverse.

01
SHOW THE NUMBER

Every claim carries a figure, a material or a tolerance. "Comfortable" is not a claim. "95A at 5.5 mm mid cell" is.

02
ONE JOKE, THEN WORK

Roughly one funny line per section. Stack two and the whole thing reads as a meme account with a Shopify.

03
PUBLISH THE FAILURES

Archived prototypes get the same typographic treatment as shipped ones. Credibility is cheaper to earn than to claim.

04
NEVER OVERSELL

No medical claims, no fake scarcity, no countdowns, no invented stock numbers. If we can't back it, we don't print it.

WE SAY
"Zoned lattice, 5.5 mm mid cell, printed in one part." "Prototype 004 split at the midfoot on day three." "It started as a joke. The tooling didn't."
WE DON'T
"Revolutionary comfort technology." "Only 3 left — don't miss out!" "Cures foot pain / improves posture / heals arches."

SHEET 09 · SIGNATURE DEVICE

BULL ON / BULL OFF

One binary that runs through the entire brand: every print is either bull on or bull off. It's the switch on the lab bench, the verdict on a prototype, the stamp on a box, and the punchline in a post — and because it can go either way in public, people believe it when it's on.

BULL ON

Inspected, numbered, stamped, shipping. The geometry did what we said it would. Gold type, seal present, serial assigned.

USE — drops that ship, passed prototypes, guarantees COLOUR — gold on black, seal visible POST FORM — "PROTO 003. BULL ON." + the spec
BULL OFF

Archived. It failed, or it wasn't good enough, and we're showing it anyway. Grey type, no seal, dashed border, the reason stated in one sentence.

USE — failed prototypes, killed concepts, recalls COLOUR — #5C5952 on black, dashed rules POST FORM — "PROTO 004. BULL OFF." + what broke
WHY IT WORKS A brand that publishes its own bull-offs gets believed about its bull-ons.

SHEET 13 · AUDIENCE SYSTEM

BULLMAKER & BULLTAKER

Two ways to be part of this, named. A BullMaker brings a mark and commissions geometry. A BullTaker takes a numbered unit from a drop. Both are earned titles with a number attached — neither is a marketing segment, and nobody gets called either one until something physical exists with their name on it.

BULLMAKER
THEY BRING
THE MARK.

Custom Bull clients. A team, a shop, a brand or one person with a symbol worth engineering into a lattice. The title lands when their sample is approved and the run starts — their mark is now geometry that gets walked on.

EARNED AT — SAMPLE APPROVED, RUN STARTED CARRIES — BULLMAKER NO. + THEIR MARK IN THE LATTICE BADGE — OUTLINE LOZENGE, GOLD RULE ON BLACK APPEARS ON — CUSTOM CERT CARD, REQUEST CONFIRMATION
"You didn't buy a print. You made one exist."
BULLTAKER
THEY TAKE
THE NUMBER.

Drop owners. One of a hundred, serialized, inspected, stamped. The title lands when the unit ships with their serial — not at checkout, because until it passes inspection there is nothing to be a taker of.

EARNED AT — UNIT INSPECTED AND SHIPPED CARRIES — SERIAL NNN / 100 + PROFILE ID BADGE — SOLID GOLD LOZENGE, KNOCKOUT BULL APPEARS ON — CERT CARD, ORDER CONFIRMATION, REPRINTS
"Number 037 of 100. It's yours and it says so."
HOW IT PAIRS WITH BULL ON / BULL OFF

Bull on / bull off is the verdict on a print. Maker and taker are the roles around a person. A BullMaker's run can still come back bull off — and if it does, they hear it first and nothing ships.

GUARDRAIL

These are not loyalty tiers, points, referral levels or a token. No leaderboard, no ranks above them, nothing to buy your way into. The moment either title can be purchased rather than earned, it becomes the thing this brand was built to avoid.

SHEET 14

NAMING ARCHITECTURE

Four levels, each with a job. The system exists so the tenth drop names itself without a meeting — and so the word "bull" doesn't end up in every slot until it stops meaning anything.

LEVEL 01 · HOUSE
BULLPRINT LAB

The company. Never versioned, never modified, never joined to a product name with a hyphen.

LEVEL 02 · FAMILY · TBC
THE FOOTBED LINE

Unnamed until drop 003 — a family needs three members before it earns a name. Breeds are the naming pool, minus BrAhMa, which belongs to the AI.

LEVEL 03 · DROP
DROP 001
₿ EDITION

Number first, always three digits. The edition name describes what makes this run different — material, mark or collaborator.

LEVEL 04 · UNIT
037 / 100
BP-10MM-001

Serial and profile ID. The serial belongs to the object; the profile belongs to the foot and can be reprinted forever.

WRITTEN OUT

FULL FORM — CERTIFICATE, PRESS, INVOICE
Drop 001 ₿ Edition
SHORT FORM — SITE, SOCIAL, CONVERSATION
bullprintlab.com/drops/001 · brahma.bullprintlab.com
URL — CONTENT ON PATHS, TOOLS ON SUBDOMAINS

Marketing content stays on paths so the search authority compounds in one place. A tool is different: BrAhMa is an application with its own deploy, its own auth and nothing to rank for, so brahma.bullprintlab.com is right — the split is the point.

RULES FOR NAMING THE NEXT ONE
Families are cattle breeds — Angus, Hereford, Charolais, Simmental. Real, physical, ownable. BrAhMa is taken.
Editions are what changed — a material, a mark, a collaborator. Never an adjective like "Pro" or "Elite".
One "bull" per name, maximum. The house has it; the family shouldn't.
Drop numbers never reset and never skip, including for runs that die. A missing 004 is a question we'd rather answer than avoid.
If a name needs explaining in the same sentence it appears, it isn't the name.

RULES
Green and red are not in this palette. Up is gold, down is deep gold — the market moves, the brand doesn't.
The timestamp is mandatory. A price with no time is a claim with no receipt.
Sats are display only. Checkout charges USD unless we ship spot pricing with a quote expiry.
Proxy the feed through our own cache. A first paint that depends on someone else's API is a dependency we didn't agree to.
aria-live="off" — it is ambient, not an announcement. It must never interrupt someone filling in the build sheet.
NEVER
No sparkline, no candles, no chart of any kind. We sell parts, not exposure.
No other assets. One line, one asset, the one we price in.
No commentary, no arrows implying a forecast, no "to the moon" copy anywhere near it.
No flashing, pulsing or ticking animation on price change. It updates silently.
Never show a stale price as if it were live. Offline is a state we display, not one we hide.

IT DOES
Reads the inspection and calls bull on or bull off, with the reason. Prescribes the geometry change in millimetres, then generates the next version. Fills a customer's build sheet from a plain description of their foot. Says "I don't know" and hands to a human.
IT NEVER
Diagnoses anything or answers a medical question. Assigns the stamp itself — BrAhMa recommends, a human signs. Quotes a price, date or stock level it can't verify from the system. Pretends to be a person, or hides that it's a model.
VOICE
Brand voice, shorter. Verdict first, then the millimetres, then the action. Two sentences beats six. No preamble, no "great question". Funny only when the situation already is — never performing. Three exchanges without resolution → bullish@bullprintlab.com.
WHY IT LANDS People understand an AI with authority over a workflow. Nobody needs another chat window.
