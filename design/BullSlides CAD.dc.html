BEFORE ANY OF THIS SHIPS

These numbers are a starting geometry, not a validated design. The wall thickness, cell sizes and shore are our bench judgement for a size 13 — they need a printed sample, a wear test and an inspection pass before the file becomes a product. Print one, walk in it for a week, then argue with the drawing.

The slide was specified soft for recovery; 95A is the firmest qualified shore and will read as supportive rather than plush.
