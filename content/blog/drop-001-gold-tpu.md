Drop 001 is live. One hundred pairs, gold TPU, printed in house.

## The mark is geometry

The rule we set before drawing anything: **no decoration that creates a pressure
point.** If branding fights comfort or manufacturability, comfort and
manufacturability win.

So the ₿ is not a logo applied to an insert. It is the region of the top surface
where no honeycomb pocket is cut. The mark sits at the top plane, flush with the
rim, standing proud of the recessed cells around it. Run a fingertip across it
and there is no step to find.

Getting there took four attempts. The first three tried to make honeycomb cells
conform to the glyph outline, and all three failed the same way: cells that get
trimmed too small have to be dropped, the mark bleeds outward into a blob, and
the counters inside the B fill in. The version that worked stopped fighting the
lattice and evaluates the surface point by point instead, so the mark is exact
at any size and the honeycomb around it is never disturbed.

## What it is not

It is a comfort and fit-experimentation insert. It is not a medical orthotic, it
is not a patient-specific orthosis, and it is not a validated medical device. We
make no medical or therapeutic claim about it. If you have a foot problem, that
is a conversation with a clinician, not with us.

What we will tell you is exactly what it measures, how it was made, and what we
got wrong on the way — which is what the rest of this journal is for.
