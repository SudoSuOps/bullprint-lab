## Why they are earned and not assigned

A title you get for paying is a receipt with a nicer font. Neither of these is
awarded at checkout.

A BullMaker becomes one when a sample has been approved and a run has started —
the point at which their mark stops being artwork and becomes geometry with a
wall thickness. A BullTaker becomes one when a unit has passed inspection and
shipped with their serial on it, because until then there is nothing to have
taken.

That means both titles can be refused, and occasionally will be. A sample that
does not clear the standard does not start a run. A unit that fails inspection
does not ship, and nobody is issued a number for it.

That is the whole point. A number that everyone gets is a customer ID.

## Not a loyalty tier

There are no points, no ranks, no leaderboard, no token, and nothing here is
purchasable. The moment either title can be bought it becomes the thing this
brand exists to avoid.

Which is why the order confirmation reads **BULLTAKER PENDING** rather than
naming you one. The serial is issued at inspection, not at checkout — and if the
unit does not pass, no number is ever assigned.
