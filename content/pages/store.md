## Bull-Grade

A joke that turned into a spec. Bull-Grade is our qualified materials list, and
it is short on purpose. We print nothing else. If a product needs a material
outside this list it needs a qualification run before it can be sold — a bench
decision, not a marketing one.

| Code | Material | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BG-01 | TPU 95A | The firm one. Footbeds, insoles and anything that has to hold an arch under load. |
| BG-02 | TPU 80A | **Suspended.** The machine vendor's own guide says 85A and softer should not be fed through our extruder at all, so this was listed as qualified against hardware that will not run it. It comes back when a coupon proves otherwise, not before. |
| BG-01b | TPU 90A | The skin-contact one. White is doctrine, not preference: a white part shows dirt and wear immediately. Bull Button and any wear surface. |
| BG-03 | PEBA | The performance material — lighter and springier than TPU. Listed, **not yet qualified on our machines**: no coupon has been run for it. Nothing ships in it until one has. |

Two entries here are honest about not being finished, and that is the point of
having a list: qualifying a material and wanting to use it are different
decisions, and only one of them is ours to make by deciding.

## Four finishes

Gold is the signature and stays the hero — insoles ship in it. Three supporting
finishes let the slides read as everyday footwear rather than trophies.

| Code | Finish | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CW-01 | Gold | The signature. Loud on purpose. Insoles. |
| CW-02 | Graphite | Equipment finish, matte near-black. Slides. |
| CW-03 | Bone | The contrast run. Limited only. |
| CW-04 | Titanium | Industrial grey for premium runs, in PEBA. |

**One finish per object.** No two-tone prints, no colour-change gimmicks. Where
a bull mark needs to contrast — a gold bull on a black slide, a black bull on a
white one — it is printed as its own part and seated flush into a pocket, so the
two colours are two prints that never meet on one plate.

Bitcoin orange is a filament we do not stock. ₿ appears as geometry, never as a
colour.

<p class="pull">Rainbow, silk, glitter and colour-shift filaments print beautifully and make the object look like a hobby print — which is the exact impression this whole brand is built to avoid.</p>

## Three drops, not a catalogue

A full-catalogue launch reads as a dropshipper. Three drops read as a lab with a
point of view. Each one gets its own week, its own story and its own verdict.

- **Drop 001 — Bull Insoles.** Live. The thesis: a lattice tuned per foot with
  no tooling cost, because there is no tool.
- **Drop 002 — Bull Slides.** In the lab. Three printed parts, no adhesive in
  the load path.
- **Drop 003 — Bull Headbands.** In the lab. The Bull Button sewn through
  spacer mesh, with its materials gate still open.

After that: reprints for worn pairs and custom runs on the same three. Owning
the file is the whole advantage — a worn pair is a repeat order rather than a
lost customer. There is no wave four written down, because writing one down is
how a lab talks itself into a catalogue.

Editions stay small because we choose to, not because we can't print more. That
distinction has to stay true or the scarcity becomes theatre.

## What we won't sell

Anything carrying a safety burden we can't back with testing. No orthotic
claims, no protective footwear, no climbing, no safety toe. A footbed that fails
is an uncomfortable afternoon; those other things fail differently. The line is
there on purpose and it does not move for a good quarter.
