MERCH 01 · SPEC SHEET · NOT FOR SALE YET

This is the production sheet, not a shop. The line is three things — insoles, slides, headbands — and every one of them is printed in this building. Merch is merch: it does not join the line and it never carries a drop number.

T-01 to T-05 are screen prints, deliberately — spot gold PMS 7555C, a halftone ring, a sixteen-inch side-seam run, an inside-collar hit. No print-on-demand service does any of that, so those five quote out to a screen printer. T-06 and T-07 are the DTG pair and the only two that could ever become print-on-demand products.

Nothing here has a price because nothing here has been made yet. When it has, it gets a buy button and this paragraph goes away. Ask about a team run.
