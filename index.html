01 — WHAT IS A BULLPRINT?

NOT A STICKER.
NOT A LOGO SLAPPED ON A PRODUCT.
THE EXPRESSION IS BUILT INTO THE PRINT.

A BullPrint integrates expression directly into geometry, material, texture and function. The ₿ is not printed on the insert — it is a region of the lattice where the cells close up, stiffen and become a symbol. Remove the graphic and you remove structure.

The identity should feel native to the object. Nothing peels, nothing wears off, and nothing about it reads as decoration bolted onto a part that was finished without it.

TEN POINTS ON QUALITY.

Five of them earn the bull — the properties we'd stake the mark on. The other five are why the first five hold.

01 A SURFACE THAT DOESN'T HOLD SWEAT

Printed TPU has a closed skin. It doesn't absorb and store moisture the way an open-cell foam footbed does, so it doesn't carry the smell of last week with it.

02 AIRFLOW DESIGNED IN, NOT ADDED

The lattice is open from the top surface through to the base, so air moves through the insert instead of around it. The holes are the structure.

03 MOISTURE DRAINS INSTEAD OF POOLING

Channels in the base membrane give water a path out and down rather than a flat plane to sit on.

04 GENUINELY WASHABLE

Soap, water, air dry, back in the shoe. No fabric top layer to fray and no glue line to soften — there is nothing to come apart in a rinse.

05 ONE CONTINUOUS PART

No adhesive, no laminate, no bonded topsheet. Delamination is the usual failure mode for layered insoles and this geometry doesn't have the joint.

06 FEEL IS TUNED, NOT GUESSED

90A for softer, 95A for firmer, plus lattice density on top of that. Two dials, same shape, different response.

07 ZONED SUPPORT ACROSS ONE PART

Cell size and wall thickness change between heel, arch and forefoot, so a single printed piece behaves differently where it should.

08 LOW-PROFILE, FLUSH FIT

Designed to sit over the shoe's own footbed without crowding the upper or lifting your heel out of the collar.

09 SERIALIZED AND REPEATABLE

Your profile is a file. The tenth print matches the first, and a replacement is a reprint, not a redesign.

10 REPAIRABLE BY REPRINT

Worn out means we run the file again in the material you liked. No mould, no minimum order, no tooling to amortise.

An antimicrobial additive is in testing and is not claimed on Drop 001. Everything above describes how the part is built and how it behaves on our bench — none of it is a health or performance claim.

BULL HEADBANDS — THE BUTTON IS THE PRINT

The spacer mesh is already the right material for a headband: open-cell, wicking, holds a stretch without a seam. What makes it ours is the Bull Button — a low-profile TPU badge, 20–30 mm, with the stitch channels printed into the geometry so it is sewn through rather than glued on, and flexes with the cloth instead of fighting it. We print the button. The skin-contact and bond questions are open and the Materials Engineer owns them, so there is no drop number on this yet.
